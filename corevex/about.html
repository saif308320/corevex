We mill machines, not marketing decks.

Corevex was founded by thermal engineers who left larger OEMs to build laptops that hold turbo without sounding like a turbine. The factory sits at 900 Silicon Way.

A short history

2016 — Three engineers prototype a vapor-chamber 15-inch in a rented garage.

2019 — First CNC graphite run. 400 units. Sold out in nine days.

2023 — Horizon silicon partnership. Sustained 120W becomes a product promise.

2026 — Pro 15 ships with Mini-LED and a 22-hour mixed battery. Still assembled in Austin.
